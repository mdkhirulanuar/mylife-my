# Prototype 0.5 Release Gate

Do not merge to `main` unless:

- Prototype 0.4 validation/regression suite remains green.
- BUD-01 through BUD-06 are green.
- No Critical/High defect remains open.
- Monthly budget data persists and is isolated by month.
- Budget values do not alter the validated Prototype 0.4 Safe to Spend or Surplus/Deficit formulas.
- UAT is accepted.
